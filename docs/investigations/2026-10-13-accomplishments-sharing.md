# Accomplishments and sharing (R7 research)

## Context

R7 asks which wins are worth keeping, how they show in the app, and how a player shares one from a phone, including "Instagram-native" stories. This spike reads what the app stores per solve today, proposes an accomplishment model, and checks current platform facts for sharing. No app code changed.

## Key findings

### What is stored per solve today

| Key | Written by | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sudoku.solves.v1` | `src/lib/solves.ts`, `game.tsx:222` | One record per game id: `id`, `difficulty`, `seconds`, `day`, `givens`. First solve only (`recordSolve`); capped at 500. |
| `sudoku.daily-solves.v1` | `src/lib/daily.ts`, `game.tsx:227` | One record per day and level: `day`, `level`, `seconds`. `dailyStreak` counts days in a row. |
| `sudoku.solved.v1` | `src/lib/home.ts` (`storeSolved`) | Ids of every finished game, uncapped. |
| `sudoku.puzzle-history.v1` | `src/lib/history.ts` | Expert bank puzzles seen and completed, including solves from before History. |
| `sudoku.replays.v1` | `src/lib/replay.ts` | Move recordings for the last 20 games, under 1 MB. `replayStats` derives numbers, notes, and fixes. |
| `sudoku.learned.v1` | `src/lib/lessons.ts` | Lesson id to the date it was learned. |

Gaps:

- **Hints are not recorded.** `applyHint` writes an ordinary move, so a replay cannot tell a hinted number from a placed one. A no-hint solve needs a new counter.
- **Fixes are derived, not stored.** The finish card computes them from the replay. Restart starts an empty replay (`replay.ts:78`), the 1 MB budget can drop replays before 20 newer games, and steps past 2,000 merge into the last one, so a replay can lose fixes. Fix counts for accomplishments must be kept live as moves happen, like hints.
- **Restart resets the clock** (`game.tsx:441` writes `seconds: 0`) but keeps the game id. A player who restarts an unsolved puzzle replays it from memory and can set a fast best time.
- **The link preview image is static.** PR #41 added committed `src/app/opengraph-image.png` and `twitter-image.png`, regenerated by hand with `pnpm build:share-image` (assets in `scripts/share-image/`). There is no image route, and the iOS app is a static export with no server, so per-solve images must be drawn on the device.

### Platform facts (checked 2026-10-13)

1. **Web Share with files works in Safari.** MDN's compatibility data lists `navigator.share()` from Safari 12.1 and the `files` member and `canShare()` from Safari 14, with iOS mirroring desktop Safari ([MDN browser-compat-data, Navigator.json](https://github.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/blob/main/api/Navigator.json)). It needs HTTPS ([MDN canShare](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/canShare)) and a user gesture ([MDN share](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/share)).
2. **WKWebView is unverified.** MDN marks the iOS WebView as mirroring Safari, but I found no primary source confirming `navigator.share({ files })` inside a Capacitor app. Test on a device before depending on it.
3. **`@capacitor/share` shares files on iOS.** Current version 8.0.3 (npm, published 2026-10-02). `ShareOptions.files` takes `file://` URLs and is "only supported on iOS and Android" ([Capacitor Share docs](https://capacitorjs.com/docs/apis/share)). The image must first be written to a file, for example with `@capacitor/filesystem`.
4. **Direct Instagram Stories is native only.** The `instagram-stories://share?source_application=<App ID>` scheme needs a Facebook App ID (required since January 2023), `instagram-stories` in `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`, and the image placed on the iOS pasteboard under `com.instagram.sharedSticker.backgroundImage` or `.stickerImage`, with optional top and bottom colors ([Meta, Sharing to Stories](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/sharing-to-stories/)). Background images are JPG or PNG, at least 720×1280, 9:16. Stickers are about 640×480. Meta documents only iOS and Android apps, so a website cannot use it, and our app would need native code in `MainViewController`. `Info.plist` has no `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes` today.
5. **Story size.** 1080×1920. Instagram covers about 250 px at the top and bottom, which leaves a central area of about 1080×1420. Meta publishes no official safe zone for organic stories. These numbers are uncited community guidance; the guide they came from no longer loads.

"Instagram-native" in practice: the share sheet path sends a PNG to Instagram, which offers Stories, Feed, and Messages. The player then edits it in Instagram's story editor. The direct scheme skips the sheet and opens the story editor with our image as the background or as a sticker on our colors. The second costs a Meta app registration and a native bridge; the first costs nothing beyond the image. That the share sheet offers Stories for an image is expected behavior I did not verify on a device.

## How it works

### Proposed accomplishment model

| Accomplishment | Evidence | Supported today? | Rule against cheap repeats | Game Center later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New best time per level | `seconds`, `difficulty` | Yes: `solves.v1`, `isNewBest` | First solve only (exists). Exclude solves after a restart. | Leaderboard, not achievement |
| First solve at a level | count per level | Yes: `levelStats` | Once per level | Achievement |
| Clean solve (no assisted hints, no fixes) | hint level reached, hints applied, fixes | No: needs new counters | Counters keyed by game id, never lowered by undo or restart | Achievement (Hard, Expert) |
| Daily solved | day, level | Yes: `daily-solves.v1` | One per day and level (exists) | None |
| Daily streak 7, 30, 100 | streak length | Yes: `dailyStreak` | Earned once per threshold; date kept | Achievement |
| Solve count 10, 50, 100, 500 | total solves | Yes: `solved.v1` plus Expert history | Ids are unique (exists) | Incremental achievement |
| Technique learned, all learned | lesson dates | Yes: `learned.v1` | Once per lesson (exists) | Achievement |

Assistance boundaries: Fill notes, highlighting, and filtered keys are bookkeeping the player chose, so they do not affect a clean solve. A hint taken to level 2 (where) or further, or an applied hint, does (decision 1), and so does a fix. Block incorrect answers is different: with it on, wrong digits are refused, so a player can guess until one is accepted and never record a fix. Whether such a solve can be clean is decision 6. Lesson practice games (`lesson-*` ids) do not earn solve accomplishments.

Storage: keep the per-solve evidence on the solve record (`hints`, `fixes`, `restarted`, all optional so old records still read), and earned milestones in `sudoku.accomplishments.v1` as id to `{ day, solveId }`. Derived wins such as first solve can be recomputed, but storing the earned date keeps them after the 500-solve cap trims History. Both keys stay under `sudoku.` so `ProgressStore` backs them up.

### Where it surfaces (show, don't explain)

- **Finish card:** the existing "Best time" sparkles chip is the pattern. Add one chip for the best accomplishment earned: a level icon for a first solve, a mark for a clean solve, and the streak count for a streak milestone. The icons are for the design directions to settle: `shield` already means Block incorrect answers, and Home's streak uses `sparkles`. A share icon button joins Replay and Play again.
- **History:** recent solve rows get a small shield for clean solves; each level card already shows the best time.
- **Daily:** a streak milestone day gets a ring in the week row and month calendar.
- **Replay:** the share button also sits in Replay's header, since a replay is the record of the solve.

### Save-to-share flow

1. The solve finishes and the evidence is saved with the record.
2. The finish card shows the chip for anything earned.
3. The player taps Share. The app draws the image on a canvas from stored data, offline, after `document.fonts.ready`.
4. `canvas.toBlob` gives a PNG. On the website: `navigator.canShare({ files })`, then `navigator.share({ files, text })`. In the iOS app: write the PNG to the cache folder and call `Share.share({ files })`.
5. If file sharing is unavailable (for example in desktop Firefox), fall back to saving or copying the image; the design doc leaves the exact fallback open.

A daily is the same puzzle for everyone, so the image shows none of the digits the player placed. Whether it shows the givens, cells shaded by when they were filled, or no board is decision 3.

### Text share, the cheap slice (not chosen)

The 3 × 3 grid is the nine boxes, colored by when each was completed (first, middle, or last third of the moves), computed from the replay. With no replay, the share leaves the grid out. It shows the shape of a solve without revealing digits, which is what Wordle's grid does.

### Recommended first slice

Record solve evidence (hints taken to level 2 or further, hints applied, fixes, restarted) on each solve, show one accomplishment chip on the finish card, and add a Share button that sends a square solve map image (decisions 3 and 5, and the [design](../plans/2026-10-13-finish-share-design.md)).

- **In:** the evidence fields, the clean-solve, first-solve, and streak-milestone chips, the image share with a fallback when file sharing is unavailable, and `sudoku.accomplishments.v1`.
- **Out:** the hint limit (decision 1), direct Instagram Stories, an accomplishments screen, and Game Center. A portrait image for Instagram Stories comes later, through the iOS app's native share. Direct Instagram Stories comes only if players ask for it (decision 4).

### Design directions (rendered October 13)

- **A. Result card:** the finish card, enlarged to 9:16 on midnight, with the givens board above it.
- **B. Solve map:** cells shaded by when they were filled as the hero, with the time and level below.
- **C. Streak calendar:** the month calendar with the streak lit, for daily and milestone shares.

## Gotchas

- Browsers require `navigator.share` to run during the tap. Drawing the image and awaiting `toBlob` first can use that up in Safari, so draw the image when the finish card appears, or test the share after the async draw on a device.
- A best time set after a restart is suspect. Mark the solve `restarted` and leave it out of best times (decision 2).
- Hint evidence must record the highest level reached, not only applied hints, so a player who sees where to look and enters the digit themselves is still marked assisted (decision 1).
- The device clock sets `day`, so streaks can be gamed. That is acceptable while progress is local; R16 must solve it before ranking.
- With Hide timer on, the finish card hides the time, and the share image should leave it out too.

## Decisions and open questions

Sean answered on October 13:

1. **Hints.** Opening a hint to see the technique's name keeps a solve clean. Going on to level 2, which shows where on the board to look, counts as assisted, and so do the walkthrough and applying a move. Sean also wants a limit on hints per puzzle: 3 by default, with a way to play with unlimited hints, which may be paid. The system is its own research item, R21. Also open: whether level 1 of a mistake hint, which says something is wrong without saying where, keeps a solve clean.
2. **Restart.** A time set after Restart does not count as a best time.
3. **Daily share.** Image B, the solve map: cells shaded by when the player filled them, with the starting numbers shown as plain dots and no digits (Sean, October 13). Render: `docs/designs/share-image-approved.png` (panel B; it predates the square shape, the flame streak mark, and the `sudoclue.app` domain).
4. **Instagram Stories.** Not now. The share sheet is enough until players ask for it.
5. **Text or image.** Image (Sean, October 13; comparison in `docs/designs/share-text-vs-image.png`). He found the emoji grid hard to read even with an explanation, and chose finish card direction A, which shares the solve map image ([design](../plans/2026-10-13-finish-share-design.md)).
6. **Block incorrect answers and Restart.** Neither can be clean (Sean, October 13): a solve with Block incorrect answers on, or after a Restart, earns no clean-solve accomplishment.
